## What it is

Feldspars are the most common minerals in the Earth's crust. Commercial deposits are those that are pure, consistent and close to customers. Sodium feldspar (albite) and potassium feldspar are the two commercial types, sold as crushed or milled product.

## Supply chain and EU dependency

Türkiye is the world's leading producer and exporter, with India, China, Italy and Spain following. The EU is itself a large producer, with Italy, Spain, Poland, Czechia, Germany and France supplying their ceramics and glass industries, and it imports the balance mainly from Türkiye. Because feldspar is a low-value bulk mineral, freight can cost as much as the material, and supply is regional rather than global. It entered the critical list in 2023 mainly on the concentration of import sources for the ceramic clusters of Italy and Spain.

## Substitution and recycling

Nepheline syenite substitutes directly in glass and ceramics, and recycled glass cullet reduces the need for virgin raw materials in glassmaking. Feldspar itself is not recovered from end-of-life ceramics.

## What to watch

Turkish export volumes and freight rates in the Mediterranean, energy costs of the EU ceramics industry, which drive demand, and permitting of quarries within the EU.
